# Dark Forest

**Status:** Early biome-unlock concept

## Progression Role

Optional forest milestone for dark oak and mushroom access.

## Build and Activation

The sketch proposes dirt and a full red mushroom, converting oak saplings to dark oak.
Changing the local biome is also suggested, but is not confirmed.

## Rewards and Unlocks

Dark oak saplings and potentially red mushrooms.
