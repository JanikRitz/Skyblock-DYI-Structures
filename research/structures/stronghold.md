# Stronghold

**Status:** Main End-access concept; activation requires a prototype

## Progression Role

Player-built gateway to the End. Generated Strongholds are disabled in the current plan.

## Build and Activation

Validate a player-built Stronghold, then convert designated Gold Blocks into End Portal
Frames. Layout, required dimensions, costs, anchor, and activation behavior remain
unverified. Candidate modules include [[research/structures/stronghold-prison]] and
[[research/structures/stronghold-library]].

## Rewards and Unlocks

Successful activation enables End access.
