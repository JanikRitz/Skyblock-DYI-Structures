# Dripstone Cave

**Status:** Early transformation concept

## Progression Role

Overworld resource milestone associated with renewable lava and obsidian production.

## Build and Activation

Convert designated stone blocks to Dripstone Blocks or pointed dripstone. Coal Block is
suggested as an anchor gated behind the Mineshaft; an Iron Block is another anchor
candidate, described as feasible after the Mineshaft.

## Rewards and Unlocks

Renewable dripstone and a route to renewable lava; potentially supports obsidian.
