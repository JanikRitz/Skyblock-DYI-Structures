# Village Variants

**Status:** Candidate biome and villager progression system

## Progression Role

Villages and biome unlocks provide villager variants, trades, and renewable resources.

## Build and Activation

The inventory lists Plains or Meadow, Desert, Savanna, Taiga or Snow, and Abandoned
variants. The current plan favors player-designated local biome regions; no village
layout or validation behavior is specified.

## Rewards and Unlocks

Biome-specific villagers and trades; possible support for saplings and difficult-to-renew
resources.
