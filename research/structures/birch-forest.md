# Birch Forest

**Status:** Early biome-unlock concept

## Progression Role

Optional Overworld biome milestone that adds birch wood and forest features.

## Build and Activation

The sketch proposes dirt, flowers or a beehive, and a fallen log. Oak saplings would be
converted to birch saplings. The beehive and fallen-log details are tentative.

## Rewards and Unlocks

Birch saplings; potentially flowers and bees.
