# Swamp Hut

**Status:** Early biome and encounter concept; also called Witch Hut in the sketches

## Progression Role

Optional swamp milestone and source of swamp-related mobs or resources.

## Build and Activation

The sketch proposes a Witch Hut area that transforms the local biome and may include a
Trial Spawner. Access could be gated by a required wood type. The drawing labels wood,
logs, slabs, mud, and dirt; spruce is tentatively marked. A Respawn Anchor is proposed,
with a question about disabling normal Witch spawns.

## Rewards and Unlocks

Potential unlocks include Witches, Frogs, a Black Cat, and possibly Mangrove-related
content.
