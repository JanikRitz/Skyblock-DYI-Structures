# Jungle Temple

**Status:** Early structure concept

## Progression Role

Optional Jungle milestone for vines, mechanisms, and jungle saplings.

## Build and Activation

The sketch proposes Mossy Cobblestone, a Dispenser, and Tripwire. The earlier inventory
calls this a Jungle Pyramid and also lists a Hopper and Piston.

## Rewards and Unlocks

Potential rewards: jungle saplings, vines, Hopper, and Piston. A Trial Vault or Trial
Spawner is proposed; the notes ask whether those should be two different structures.
