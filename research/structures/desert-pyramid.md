# Desert Pyramid

**Status:** Early structure concept

## Progression Role

Optional desert milestone for sand, sandstone, and gated loot.

## Build and Activation

The sketch proposes sandstone and TNT. Exact dimensions, anchor, and validation rules
are not specified.

## Rewards and Unlocks

Trial Vault rewards considered: enchanted books, sugar cane, and iron or gold horse
armor. The broader inventory also lists sand, sandstone, diamonds, TNT, and cactus as
possible rewards or renewable resources.
