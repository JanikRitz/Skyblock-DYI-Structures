# Nether Bastion

**Status:** Early Nether progression concept

## Progression Role

Nether milestone for Blackstone, Gold, Piglin Brutes, and Netherite-related progression.

## Build and Activation

The sketch suggests building a small portion of a real Bastion and dividing the rewards
into two tiers. Exact anchor and validation rules are open.

## Rewards and Unlocks

One tier may use a Gold Block associated with a Piglin Brute encounter. A Trial Spawner
and Vaults are proposed for Netherite resources and the Smithing Template. The wider
progression notes also associate Blackstone and Gold with bartering or zombified-piglin
resource farms.
