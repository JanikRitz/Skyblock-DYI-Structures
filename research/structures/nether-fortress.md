# Nether Fortress

**Status:** Main Nether progression candidate

## Progression Role

Nether progression for Blaze Rods, Nether Wart, and access toward the End.

## Build and Activation

The concept is to build functional modules rather than generate a complete Fortress.
Candidate modules are [[research/structures/blaze-spawner]] and
[[research/structures/fortress-crossroads]].

## Rewards and Unlocks

Potential resources include Nether Wart and Blaze Rods, with Wither Skeleton encounters
and gated Fortress loot in the crossroads module.
