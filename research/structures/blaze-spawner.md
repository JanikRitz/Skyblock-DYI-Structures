# Blaze Spawner

**Status:** Early Nether encounter concept

## Progression Role

Nether milestone intended to follow the shape of a normal Blaze-spawner structure as
closely as practical.

## Build and Activation

The sketch proposes a Nether Brick base layer and Nether Brick Fences. A Respawn Anchor
or Glowstone is questioned as the anchor; neither is a settled requirement.

## Rewards and Unlocks

Blaze encounter and the usual Fortress progression resources are candidates.
