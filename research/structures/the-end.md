# The End

**Status:** Main progression concept

## Progression Role

Final stage after the player-built Stronghold and End access.

## Build and Activation

The progression note suggests starting with one End structure. The Dragon Egg is proposed
as an anchor for End Stone and Chorus-related renewal; the details are untested.

## Rewards and Unlocks

End resources, with End Cities potentially providing Elytra, Shulkers, Purpur, and Chorus
Fruit.
