# Mushroom Island

**Status:** Early biome-unlock concept

## Progression Role

Optional biome milestone for Mycelium and mushroom access.

## Build and Activation

The sketch proposes Dirt and a full Brown Mushroom, then converting Dirt to Mycelium.
Changing the local biome is also suggested.

## Rewards and Unlocks

Mycelium and brown mushrooms; a Mushroom Fields biome or its mob access may be part of
the unlock.
